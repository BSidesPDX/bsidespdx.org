### Sign up to Volunteer

If you would like to sign up to volunteer for this years event, head over to [this page](../events/2025/volunteer).

### Expectations

Volunteers (like participants) are expected to abide by the [Code of Conduct](./code-of-conduct.html).

### Roles and Opportunities

* Public attendees are encouraged to attend any/all BSides PDX board meetings. Minutes and information about when/where we meet virtually are shared through the <a href="https://groups.google.com/forum/#!forum/bsidespdx">BSides PDX Google Group</a>.

* Every year at the event, we need badge coders, speaker and workshop coordinators, party planners, swag coordinators, and more. Fill out the [volunteer form](../events/2025/volunteer) and let us know if you want to help with any of these.

* Additionally, we have lots of longer-term, off-season, and year round volunteer opportunities such as sponsor coordination, social media coordination, web page maintenance, and AV skills. Reach out by [email](mailto:info@bsidespdx.org) if you have any specific interests or want to learn more
