BSidesPDX 2025 will be held on Fri Oct 24 & Sat Oct 25

We'll be back at Smith Memorial Student Union, but we should have even more space available to us!
Registration is open! Like past years, we have name-your price registration and tshirts - but register early because free registration and tshirt orders will close a month before the event!
Interested in presenting? The CFP is open too! We're looking for a broad selection of submissions covering different parts of the infosec and hacking space, as well as different levels from newcomer to expert. We're pushing hard for more short 20 minute talks this year. Chances are, if you were at BSidesPDX last year you've learned something in the past year that we'd love for you to share. Take a look at the CFP for more details.

About BSides PDX

BSides Portland is a tax-exempt charitable 501(c)(3) organization founded with the mission to cultivate the Pacific Northwest information security and hacking community by creating local inclusive opportunities for learning, networking, collaboration, and teaching.

If you have questions, comments, or are interested in being more involved, don't hesitate to reach out to info [@] bsidespdx.org.
